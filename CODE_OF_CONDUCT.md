# Code of Conduct — EMO-X

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include
demonstrating empathy and kindness, respecting differing opinions, giving and
gracefully accepting constructive feedback, and focusing on what is best for
the community. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, trolling, insulting
comments, publishing others' private information without permission, and any
other conduct inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported to the project owner (SE-Emam) via a private channel, such as a
GitHub private vulnerability report or direct message to the maintainers.
All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and
maintainers will respect the privacy and security of the reporter.

## Scope and Full Text

This is a short summary. EMO-X adopts the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html),
whose full text — including detailed standards, enforcement responsibilities,
and guidelines — is authoritative. Where this summary and the full text
differ, the full text governs.
